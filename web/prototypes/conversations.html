Conversations: Chat and Sessions as one surface

One list, one view. A conversation is Read-only or Can edit, and you switch it in the header without starting over. Click through the scenarios, or use the controls directly.

Conversations
Project: Novara
Mode is per conversation, switched by later turns. History carries over. The switch is a divider in the transcript, so a reader can see which answers were given read-only and which turns could edit.
Switching to Can edit asks once. The sheet says what changes: writes go through the approval panel, a budget starts, and the checkout is locked to this conversation. Switching back needs no confirm.
One editing conversation per project. When another conversation holds the checkout, Can edit is disabled with a link to it. Read-only conversations are never blocked.
Both modes have the same reading tools and draft_task. A read-only conversation that finds a bug drafts the task itself; the card shows it landed in the approval queue, and Approve here is the same action as in the project's queue.
System conversations are hidden by default. Proposals, intake, infer-spec and Draft as task still create rows; they sit behind "Show system conversations".

Allow this conversation to edit?

  • Writes and commands need your approval, one at a time, in a panel above the composer.
  • It works in the Novara checkout itself, not a copy. No other conversation can edit Novara until this one goes back to Read-only.
  • A budget starts: $2.00 per conversation today.
  • It still cannot commit, merge or push. Work that grows task-shaped becomes a drafted task.
